Privacy Policy for Sadaqah Jariyah

Last updated: July 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sadaqah Jariyah Android application ("the App") handles user information. The App is developed and published by Dr. Mohammedelnagi Mohammed as a Quran reading, audio, tafsir, and dedication application.

Information We Collect

The App does not require users to create an account, sign in, submit a name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

We do not knowingly collect, store, sell, or share personal user data. The App does not include advertising SDKs and does not use user profiling for advertising.

App Permissions

The App uses Internet access to load or stream Quran audio, tafsir videos, and related Islamic content links. The App does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, calendar, or phone call permissions.

Third-Party Content and Services

Some audio, tafsir, or video content may be provided through external services such as YouTube or other publicly available content sources. When users open or play such content, those third-party services may process standard technical information according to their own privacy policies, such as IP address, device/browser information, or playback activity. We do not control those third-party services.

Local App Data

The App may store basic preferences locally on the user's device, such as selected language or reading position, to improve the reading experience. This information stays on the device and is not sent to us.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended as an Islamic educational and Quran application. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal user data with third parties. Any interaction with external content providers is governed by the privacy policy of that external provider.

Data Security

Because the App does not collect personal information on our servers, there is no personal user database maintained by us. Users should still keep their own devices secure and updated.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect or store personal user data on our servers, there is no server-side personal data for us to delete. Users can delete local app preferences and locally stored app data by clearing the App's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes or when legal or platform requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the GitHub repository issue page: https://github.com/elnagiappdev-maker/sadaqah-jariyah-content/issues
